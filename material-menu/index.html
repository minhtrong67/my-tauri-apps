Good to know

Where do my custom commands appear?

Classic registry entries show up under “Show more options” (Shift+F10) in the compact Windows 11 menu. Turn on the classic full menu in the app to see them right away.

How is this different from the built-in “Customize menu”?

Windows 11 with update KB5127936 is rolling out its own Customize menu entry. Material Menu works through the registry, so it also works on builds that do not have that update, and it adds your own commands and backups.

Is it safe? Can I undo everything?

Everything is stored for your user only. Use Reset in the Backup page to remove all custom commands, show hidden items and return to the compact menu.

Do I need administrator rights?

No. The installer and the app only write to your own user profile and registry hive.
