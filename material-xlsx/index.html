Features

Everything you expect from a spreadsheet

Familiar Excel-style tools, wrapped in a clean Material Design 3 interface that is a pleasure to use all day.

Open & save .xlsx

Styles, merged cells, column widths, frozen panes, filters, notes and hyperlinks are kept when you save.

200+ functions

Maths, statistics, financial (PMT, NPV, IRR), dates, text and lookup — including XLOOKUP, SUMIFS and more.

Conditional formatting

Highlight rules, colour scales, data bars, top / bottom N and duplicate values, with a rule manager.

Animated charts

Column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut and scatter. Drag, resize and edit them right on the sheet.

Data validation

Dropdown lists, number, date and text-length rules with friendly input messages and error alerts.

Data tools

Multi-level sort, filter, remove duplicates, text to columns, paste special, format as table.

Your colour, your mode

Light, dark or follow the system, with Material You colours generated from any seed colour.

English & Tiếng Việt

The interface automatically matches your system language and can be switched at any time.

Window your way

Remember the last window size, or always start maximized — one click in Settings.

Fast and light

Virtualised grid with a million rows, native Tauri shell — starts in a blink and stays smooth.

Private by design

Works entirely offline. Your files never leave your computer — no account, no tracking.

Keyboard friendly

Excel-style shortcuts: Ctrl+Z/Y, Ctrl+Arrows, Ctrl+K, Ctrl+Alt+V, F2, Ctrl+; and many more.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?
Yes. Material Xlsx is open source under the MIT license — free for personal and commercial use.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Everything runs on your computer. Your spreadsheets are never uploaded anywhere.
Can I open files made in Microsoft Excel?
Yes. It reads and writes .xlsx files, keeping styles, merges, filters, frozen panes, notes, hyperlinks, conditional formats and data validation. Charts are restored when you reopen the file in Material Xlsx.
Which languages are supported?
English and Vietnamese. The app detects your system language on first launch and you can change it any time.
Windows says “unknown publisher”. Is it safe?
The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may show a warning. Choose “More info → Run anyway”, or build the app yourself from the open-source code.
