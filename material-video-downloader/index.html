FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no tracking.

Which sites are supported?

Direct video files and HLS streams work out of the box. For video platforms and social networks the app installs yt-dlp for you automatically.

Do I need to install ffmpeg myself?

No. On Windows the app downloads it on first launch. It merges separate video and audio tracks (for the highest qualities) and converts to MP3. On macOS and Linux install it with your package manager.

Is downloading videos legal?

Only download content you own or have permission to save, and respect each website’s terms of service. You are responsible for how you use the app.

How do I install the extension?

Unzip the file, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, choose “Load unpacked” and select the folder. Keep the desktop app running.
