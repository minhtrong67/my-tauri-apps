Everything you need to enjoy music

Thoughtful details, smooth animations, tiny footprint.

Material You themes

Light, dark or system mode, 18 Material colours, custom colour and dynamic colour from the cover art.

Equalizer & sound

10-band equalizer with presets, pre-amp, playback speed and a live visualizer.

Smart library

Auto-scans your Music folder and watches your folders for changes, reads tags and covers, M3U import / export and instant search.

Synced lyrics

Reads .lrc files and embedded lyrics, can look them up online on request, highlights the current line and seeks on click.

Mini player & tray

Always-on-top mini player, system tray controls, media keys and sleep timer.

English & Vietnamese

Detects your system language automatically and remembers your window size or opens maximized.

Gapless & crossfade

The next song is preloaded and takes over with no silence, with optional crossfade and ReplayGain loudness matching.

Tag editor & smart playlists

Fix title, artist and album right inside the file, and let Most played, New and Lossless playlists fill themselves.

Global media keys

Play, pause and skip from your keyboard even when the app is in the background or the tray.

Statistics & duplicates

See your top songs, artists and listening time, and merge duplicate songs while keeping the best copy.

Your own smart playlists

Build playlists from rules such as genre, year, bitrate or favorites, and keep them up to date automatically.

Safe & backed up

Atomic saves, automatic daily backups and recovery if a file is ever damaged.

Your shortcuts

Change every keyboard shortcut and control playback from any app with global hotkeys.

Choose your speakers

Pick the audio output, fade out with the sleep timer and tune lyric timing per song.

Plays the formats you already have

Tags and cover art are read from every format below. Drop in a file or a whole folder.

MP3FLACWAVOGGOpusM4AAAC
