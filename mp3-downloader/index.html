FAQ

Good to know

How does Spotify work?
Spotify doesn't allow direct downloads. The app reads the track info (title, artist, album, cover) from the link and finds matching audio on YouTube, then tags the MP3 for you.
Windows says "unrecognized app" (SmartScreen)
The installer isn't code-signed yet, which is normal for small open-source apps. Click "More info" → "Run anyway". You can also read and build the source yourself.
A download fails with HTTP 403 or "sign in"
Update yt-dlp in Settings → Tools, install Deno, or choose "Use browser cookies" under Network. These fix almost every YouTube error.
Is it legal?
Only download content you own or have permission to use, and respect the terms of each platform and your local laws. The app is a tool; responsibility is yours.
Where are my files?
In your Downloads folder by default. Change it, add per-artist folders and name templates in Settings → Downloads.
