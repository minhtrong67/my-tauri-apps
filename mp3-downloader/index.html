Everything you need, nothing you don't

A light, beautiful and easy desktop app for saving the music you love.

Cover art & tags

Artist, title, album, year and square cover art are embedded in every file.

YouTube, TikTok & Spotify

Paste a link from any of them. Spotify tracks are matched to the best audio on YouTube.

Up to 320 kbps

Choose Best (VBR V0) or 320, 256, 192 and 128 kbps.

Smart queue

Paste several links at once. Downloads run two at a time with progress, retry and cancel.

Material You themes

Light, dark or system, with 16 Material colors and any custom color.

Your language

English and Tiếng Việt, picked automatically from your device.

Remembers your window

Reopen at your last size, or always start maximized.

Free & open source

Just three steps

From link to MP3 in seconds.

1

Paste a link

Copy a YouTube, TikTok or Spotify link and paste it — the app reads the track info.

2

Pick a quality

Choose Best or a fixed bitrate. The app downloads and converts to MP3.

3

Enjoy your music

Open the folder from the notification. Cover art shows up in Explorer and your player.

Ready to try it?

Get the Windows installer. On first launch the app can install yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno for you.
