Features

Everything you need to fix your text

A small, quick app that does one job really well.

19 letter cases

UPPERCASE, Title Case, camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case, URL slug and more, with Vietnamese-aware diacritics.

Live as you type

The result updates instantly. Copy it with one click or Ctrl + Shift + C.

All cases at a glance

See every conversion at once and click any row to copy it.

9 text tools

Remove duplicate lines, sort A to Z, trim, join, number lines and more.

Material 3 themes

Light, dark or system, with 18 Material colors, your own custom color and a choice of fonts.

English and Vietnamese

Detects your device language automatically. Switch any time in Settings.

Remembers your window

Reopen at the size you left it, or always start maximized. You choose.

Private and offline

Your text never leaves your computer. No account, no tracking.

Themes

Make it yours

Pick a theme color and this whole page recolors, just like the app does with Material Color Utilities.
