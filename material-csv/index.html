Features

Everything a CSV editor should do

From quick fixes to serious data cleaning, without leaving one fast, friendly window.

Spreadsheet-style editing

Click, drag and type. Formula bar, copy and paste that works with Excel, fill down and right, and a row-details form for the selected record.

Undo and redo

200 steps for every action, with unsaved changes tracked per tab.

Built for big files

Virtualised rendering keeps scrolling smooth with hundreds of thousands of rows.

Find and replace

Regular expressions, whole cell, match case, search in selection.

Filter and sort

Quick filter row, multi-condition filters and non-destructive sorting.

Data tools

Trim, change case, remove duplicates and empty rows, column statistics.

Tabs and drag & drop

Open many files at once, drop them anywhere, jump back to recent files.

Open and export anything

CSV, TSV and Excel in. CSV, TSV, JSON, Excel, Markdown and HTML out. Encoding, delimiter and line endings are detected and changeable.

Dynamic color

Light, dark or system, with a Material 3 palette from any seed color.

English and Vietnamese

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Material CSV free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no tracking. Your files never leave your computer.

Can it open Excel files and big files?

It opens .xlsx, .xls and .ods (you pick the sheet) and handles hundreds of thousands of rows thanks to virtualised rendering.

Does it support Vietnamese text and other encodings?

Yes. Encoding and delimiter are detected automatically (UTF-8, UTF-16, Windows-1258 and more) and can be changed from the status bar. New files use UTF-8 with BOM so Excel shows accents correctly.

Can I change the colors and language?

Open Settings (the gear icon): choose light, dark or system, pick any Material seed color and contrast level, and switch between English and Vietnamese. The app follows your system language by default.

Does it remember my window size?

Yes. In Settings → Window choose between remembering the size and position, or always opening maximized.
